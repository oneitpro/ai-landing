# AI Discovery Meeting Guide

## Meeting objective

Understand how the customer's people work, how AI is already being used, what information is involved, and which opportunities are worth investigating. This is a discovery conversation, not a product demonstration.

## Suggested 30-minute flow

1. **Context — 3 minutes:** confirm attendees, business priorities, and why the conversation is useful now.
2. **Current use — 7 minutes:** identify tools, people, frequency, and actual use cases.
3. **Data and governance — 7 minutes:** understand information being entered, approval, oversight, and concerns.
4. **Workflows and opportunities — 9 minutes:** identify repetitive work, bottlenecks, service challenges, and measurable outcomes.
5. **Next steps — 4 minutes:** recap findings and explain the complimentary review.

## Current use

- Which AI tools are employees using?
- Who is using them and how often?
- What are they using them for?
- Is usage company-approved or informal?
- Which results have been useful? Which have not?

## Data and governance

- What company, customer, client, employee, or confidential information may be entered?
- Are employees clear about what should not be shared?
- Are AI-generated results reviewed by a person?
- Are approved tools or accounts defined?
- Are security, privacy, retention, intellectual-property, or accuracy concerns present?
- Does the organization already have an acceptable-use or AI policy?

Do not provide legal advice or promise compliance. Identify questions that may require the customer's legal or compliance counsel.

## Work and opportunity discovery

- Which tasks are repetitive, manual, slow, or error-prone?
- Where do employees repeatedly search for the same information?
- Which documents are summarized, compared, classified, or re-keyed?
- Where do customer inquiries need routing, follow-up, or consistent answers?
- Which systems hold the necessary information?
- What would a useful improvement look like?
- How would the customer measure value: time, quality, speed, capacity, risk reduction, or customer experience?

## Qualification notes

For each opportunity, capture:

- business problem and current process
- people affected and process owner
- source systems and data sensitivity
- frequency and volume
- current cost or friction
- required human review
- technical dependencies
- expected result and measurable value
- recommended next step: policy, training, process change, existing software, automation, AI solution, or no action

## Close

Explain that One I.T. Pro will prepare the complimentary AI Usage & Policy Review and practical recommendations. Do not imply that every discovery produces a paid project.
