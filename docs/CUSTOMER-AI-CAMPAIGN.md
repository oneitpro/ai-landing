# Customer AI Campaign

## Purpose

Invite existing One I.T. Pro customers to an **AI Discovery & Readiness Assessment** so One I.T. Pro can understand current AI usage, governance concerns, and practical opportunities for automation or implementation.

This is a customer-relationship campaign, not cold prospecting. Do not mix its audience with Prospeo, `saleslawfirms`, or law-firm acquisition cohorts.

## Canonical campaign identifier

`customer_ai_discovery_2026q4`

## Offer

- initial discovery conversation to identify the business problem, current AI use, goals, risks, and opportunities
- Microsoft Teams/online or at the customer's office when appropriate
- follow-up review of relevant business processes and AI usage, including appropriate audits
- written practical recommendations and AI-use and governance policy guidance
- practical recommendations
- no charge and no obligation

The meeting is not a promise of a paid project. Opportunities identified during discovery may later become separate paid proposals.

## Audience preparation

Prepare an initial wave of approximately 20–25 contacts from **One IT Pro Customer Communications**. Prefer one decision-maker or appropriate primary contact per customer for the first wave.

Selection considerations:

- healthy current customer relationship
- appropriate decision-maker/contact
- local enough for an in-person session where useful
- sufficient organizational activity or processes for discovery
- no current billing or service dispute
- industry diversity where practical

Customer status remains authoritative. The audience requires Marketing approval before sending.
