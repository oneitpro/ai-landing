Common questions

A practical first conversation.

Do we need to already use AI?

No. Discovery can begin by identifying business processes where AI may—or may not—provide practical value.

What if employees are already using ChatGPT or other AI tools?

That's exactly why an AI usage review is useful. We'll discuss what tools are being used, what they're being used for, and what information may be entering those systems.

Do you only work with Microsoft Copilot?

No. One I.T. Pro begins with the business problem rather than a specific AI product.

Can you create an AI usage policy?

Yes. AI usage and data-governance guidance are part of One I.T. Pro's AI consulting services. Our guidance is technology and governance support, not legal advice.

Can One I.T. Pro build AI solutions?

Yes. When discovery identifies a suitable opportunity, One I.T. Pro can design and implement AI assistants, automation, integrations, and other AI-enabled workflows.

Can the meeting be online?

Yes. Meetings may be held online or, when appropriate, at the customer's office.

What is included in the AI Discovery & Readiness Assessment?

It begins with a discovery conversation about the business problem, current AI use, goals, risks, and opportunities. We then review relevant processes and AI usage, including appropriate audits, and follow with written practical recommendations and AI-use and governance policy guidance.
